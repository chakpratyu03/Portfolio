About Me

Turning thoughts into content, and content into leads

I'm a content strategist and marketing writer with 7+ years of experience, someone who turns thoughts into articles, and articles into leads. My craft lies in growth focused, SaaS driven writing built for both B2B and B2C audiences.

Over the years I've worn many hats: agency writer, B2B/B2C content specialist, freelance content marketer and in house strategist, across EdTech, FinTech, SaaS, CRM & ERP, real estate, pricing pages and digital marketing. Each hat taught me a slightly different way to listen to a brief and answer it in the right voice.

Beyond the boardroom brief lives a quieter kind of writing I keep just for myself. Corporate copy pays the bills and sharpens my discipline, while my Medium essays, written as "Pratzz", are where the unfiltered thoughts get to wander, alongside my LinkedIn newsletter, Words to World with Pratzz.

My Work Process

How a project moves

The same six steps, whether it's a 600-word FAQ or a 3,000-word pillar blog.

1

Brief & discovery

I start by understanding the goal, the audience and the brand voice. Every piece begins with a proper conversation, not a blank page.

2

Research

I dig into the topic through keyword analysis, competitor mapping and SERP research using Semrush, Ahrefs and Surfer SEO. This is where the story finds its shape.

3

Outline & draft

I build a clear structure, then write the first draft with the reader firmly in mind. This stage is raw on purpose, giving ideas room to breathe before they get polished.

4

Proofread & benchmark

I proofread thoroughly using Grammarly, Claude and ChatGPT, and check the piece against competitor content to see where it stands on depth and clarity.

5

Edit & optimise

I self-edit for flow and clarity, then optimise the piece for search engines and AI answer engines (GEO/AIO), so it performs wherever readers find it.

6

Client review & handover

I share the draft, listen closely to feedback and revise until it reads exactly as it should. Then comes the final polish and a publish-ready file, delivered on time.
